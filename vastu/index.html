Vastu Consultant in Gurugram

Vastu-aware design can be part of a thoughtful planning conversation. Shape My Home helps homeowners in Gurugram consider orientation and room use alongside comfort, function and the realities of their home.

Bring your priorities into the conversation

People approach Vastu with different questions and levels of familiarity. Start by explaining what matters to you, whether you are assessing a home before purchase, planning a new layout or looking for practical changes in an existing space. Share the available plan, the direction of the site or entrance if known, and any restrictions that cannot be changed. This context makes it possible to discuss options clearly rather than relying on assumptions.

Vastu guidance should be considered alongside building conditions, safety, accessibility, ventilation, daylight and household needs. A design decision that looks simple on a diagram may affect circulation, privacy or the location of existing services. In Gurugram homes, the specific plot, apartment or building layout will determine what can reasonably be adjusted. Ask which suggestions are optional and which depend on further site information.

Use a practical planning framework

Record what is known

Before a consultation, gather a measured plan, approximate north direction, door and window locations, fixed service points and a short list of your concerns. Mark which walls or elements are structural or shared, if that information is available. Separate changes you are considering from changes that are already underway, and note who needs to approve decisions in a multi-owner or apartment setting.

Evaluate choices in context

Discuss each recommendation in terms of feasibility and its effect on daily use. A room's intended activity, natural light, ventilation, furniture placement and circulation remain important design considerations. If an ideal adjustment is not feasible, ask about reasonable alternatives rather than compromising essential building functions. No single layout can replace a proper review of the actual space and its requirements.
