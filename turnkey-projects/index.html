Turnkey Projects in Gurugram

Planning a new home or a major renovation in Gurugram? Shape My Home brings design decisions and project coordination together, helping you move from an agreed brief to a more considered, well-managed home.

A coordinated approach to your home project

A turnkey home project brings the key decisions into one connected plan. Rather than treating layouts, interiors, materials and on-site work as separate conversations, Shape My Home helps you define the scope, priorities and sequence before work begins. This is useful for new apartments, independent homes and renovations where several rooms or trades need to come together.

Every property has its own constraints. An apartment in a Gurugram high-rise may have different access, service and fit-out requirements from a villa or an independent house. The first step is to understand the property, your needs and any building or society guidelines that affect the work.

What can be included in a turnkey project?

Depending on the agreed brief, a residential project may bring together services such as:

  • Space planning, floor layouts and interior design
  • Kitchen, bathroom, furniture and storage design
  • Material and finish selection for the rooms in scope
  • Coordination of project stages and execution requirements
  • Review of work against the agreed drawings, details and selections

The exact deliverables, responsibilities, materials and exclusions should be recorded in a project scope before work starts. Approval, structural and specialist requirements must be assessed for the specific property and handled by appropriately qualified professionals.

Plan the project before execution

Set priorities and define the scope

Start with the rooms, functions and outcomes that matter most. A clear brief helps distinguish essential work from optional upgrades and gives the design and execution teams a shared reference. Establish which decisions need your approval and how changes to the agreed scope will be reviewed.

Coordinate layouts, materials and site conditions

Check that layouts work with the measured space, existing services, access routes and practical clearances. Review finish samples in context and confirm availability before finalising selections. In Gurugram, property-specific fit-out or society rules can also affect delivery, so identify those requirements early with the relevant building management.

Keep communication clear through delivery

Agree on responsibilities, decision points and a communication routine before work begins. Progress reviews help surface questions while there is still time to resolve them. Timelines and budgets depend on the size of the project, site conditions, chosen materials and approvals; these should be discussed against the actual scope rather than assumed in advance.
